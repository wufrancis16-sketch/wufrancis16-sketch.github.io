Digital Supply Chain Management for a Guangzhou Trading Firm

A Guangzhou apparel-accessory exporter fought old software, customs chaos, and FX pain. Mobile approval, auto FX, and order tracing fixed it.

Company Profile / Background

The company is a Guangzhou supply-chain manager handling production, trade, and export of apparel accessories, clothing, handbags, shoulder bags, backpacks, and waist bags. It runs a make-to-buy model: take the overseas order, sign a sales order, then place purchase orders with internal units or outside suppliers, and clear customs through a freight forwarder. Serving international buyers means juggling customs codes, multiple currencies, and tight response times. A missed approval or a mis-keyed customs code does not just cost time; it can hold a container at the border and a relationship with a buyer who will not wait.

Core Challenges

Urgent Need for Mobile Work and Approval

The legacy T+ 13.0 lacked a mobile app and OA approval, so managers traveling could not approve documents, stalling flows and slowing order response, especially painful on urgent orders.

Customs-Code and Item Confusion

One customs code covered several different product names, so declaration and purchase documents mismatched. A single code could span many bag styles the system could not tell apart, muddying declarations and risking customs queries.

Complex Multi-Currency FX Work

USD, EUR, and HKD settlements forced finance to maintain rates and compute exchange gains by hand, slow, error-prone, and unable to show true foreign-currency value in real time.

Hard Traceability, Unclear Profit

Sales orders could not be traced back to linked purchase and receipt documents, and with no cost allocation, true per-deal profit was invisible, undermining pricing and decisions.

How the System Solved It

The platform's intelligent processing and integrated finance built an efficient import-export management system.

Mobile Office and AI Document Entry

The mobile app covers entry, approval, and query on the go, so managers act anywhere. AI document creation reads a customer order from a photo or PDF and generates the sales order fast. Flexible multi-level approval with real-time alerts keeps flows clean.

Multi-Currency with Auto FX

A full foreign-currency account system handles USD, EUR, and HKD. Daily rates auto-fetch with manual override for timeliness and accuracy. Foreign receipts and payments sync to the right currency account and exchange gains compute automatically, cutting manual error.

Make-to-Buy Tracking and Traceability

The complete chain, sales order to purchase order to receipt to shipment, is clear and controlled. Document history enables two-way lookup from a sales order back to its purchase and receipt, and the make-to-buy tracking table shows all document numbers and progress at a glance.

Document Two-Way Lookup in Practice

When a customer questions one line on an invoice, the team can open the sales order and walk backward to the exact purchase and receipt that produced it, in seconds rather than an afternoon. That traceability turns a potential dispute into a confident, evidence-backed answer.

Smart Data Query for Decisions

An AI natural-language query returns sales data as charts and reports instantly. Multi-dimensional analysis across orders, inventory, and finance supports fast, evidence-based decisions.

AI Query Lowers the Reporting Barrier

Before, only someone fluent in report builders could pull a number, so questions piled up on the one analyst. Now a manager can simply ask in plain language and get the chart, which means decisions are made on data the day they are needed, not the week after.

Measurable Results

Faster Mobile Approval

Mobile app and AI entry let managers capture orders on the road, killing lag and missed orders and lifting satisfaction. An urgent overseas order no longer waits for the boss to return to the office; it is approved at the airport and moving the same hour.

Automated Foreign-Currency Management

Auto rate fetch and FX computation cut finance workload and sharpen accuracy, supporting fast export growth. The finance team stops maintaining a rate table by hand and instead spends its time reading what the currency movement actually means for margin.

Stronger Traceability and Decisions

Full make-to-buy visibility and two-way document lookup make tracing simple; AI queries put timely data in managers' hands for smarter calls. The combination means a question that used to need a meeting now needs a search.

Conclusion

For an export trader, the edge is responsiveness and clarity. Mobile approval, automatic FX, and end-to-end tracing turned scattered international operations into one controllable system. The same setup also gives overseas partners a consistent view of order status, which shortens the trust-building cycle with new buyers.
