Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) for Global Rollouts

For a going-global company, TCO extends beyond license fees to multi-entity, multi-country deployment. Here's what actually goes in:

High

Software licenses / subscriptions

The visible cost — usually 20–40% of true TCO

High

Implementation services

Typically 0.5x–3x of first-year license cost

Medium-High

Customization & integrations

Varies wildly; budget 20–50% of implementation cost

Medium

Data migration

$10K–$100K+ depending on data quality and volume

Medium

Training & change management

Often underbudgeted; plan 10–15% of total project cost

Medium

Ongoing support & maintenance

15–22% of license cost annually for on-premise; included in most cloud

Medium

Infrastructure (on-premise only)

Servers, backup, DR, security — $20K–$200K+/year

Hidden

Internal IT staff time

Allocate 0.5–2 FTE for ERP administration post-go-live
